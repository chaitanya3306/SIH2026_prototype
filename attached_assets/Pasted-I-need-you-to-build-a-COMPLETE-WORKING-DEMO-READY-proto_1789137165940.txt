==================================================
PROJECT
==================================================

Project title:

Unified Online Verification & Lifecycle Management System for Weighing and Measuring Instruments

Core idea:

Every legally regulated weighing/measuring instrument gets a persistent digital identity, called a DIGITAL INSTRUMENT PASSPORT.

The platform manages the complete lifecycle:

Registration
→ Verification Application
→ Officer/GATC Assignment
→ Scheduling
→ Field Verification
→ Digital Certificate
→ QR Verification
→ Expiry Monitoring
→ Re-verification
→ Audit / Enforcement

The system should support multiple instrument categories:

- Weighing Scales
- Platform Scales
- Weighbridges
- Petrol/Diesel Fuel Dispensers
- LPG/CNG/LNG Dispensers
- Other configurable measuring instruments

IMPORTANT:
Do not claim that all instrument categories have identical legal verification procedures.
This software manages configurable workflows and records verification results entered by authorized users.
